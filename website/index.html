Why it exists

Understanding without the source. Ask for the mechanism. Get observations, not guesses.

reaharness is the agent layer for reverse engineering work: typed tools, evidence in every result, unknowns called out instead of papered over. MCP for your IDE agent. CLI when you want the loop yourself.

Every answer carries proof.

Observations, unknowns, and budgets travel with the payload. Missing coverage stays unknown.
